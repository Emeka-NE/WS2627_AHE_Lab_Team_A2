# Milestone 2 - Outline


## 1. Research Question

To what extent do modern hardware description languages such as Chisel, SpinalHDL, Clash, and Amaranth improve hardware design productivity compared to traditional VHDL, and what challenges limit their adoption in industry?

## 2. Proposed Paper Structure

### 2.1. Abstract
- Brief overview of the research, approach, and findings

### 2.2. Introduction
- Motivation for modern hardware description languages
- Research objectives and productivity challenges

### 2.3. Background and Related Work
- Traditional VHDL and modern hardware construction languages
- Embedded DSLs, parameterization, and type systems
- Related research on modern HDLs

### 2.4. Main Part - Analysis and Comparison
- Overview of Chisel, SpinalHDL, Clash, and Amaranth
- Comparison with VHDL in terms of productivity, reusability, and language features
- Examples of hardware generation and verification support

### 2.5. Discussion / Evaluation
- Advantages and limitations of modern HDLs
- Toolchain compatibility and industrial adoption challenges

### 2.6. Conclusion
- Summary of the main findings
- Future outlook
